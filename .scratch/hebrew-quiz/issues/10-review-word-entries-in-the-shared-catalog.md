# 10: Review Word Entries in the Shared Catalog

**What to build:** Reviewers can create and publish Word Entries with their own word, meaning, and hints through the same localized editorial workflow used by Questions.

**Blocked by:** 06 (Publish localized questions with shared fields), 08 (Share and replace catalog questions)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A reviewer can author and review a Word Entry with a target word, meaning, and optional hints. Word Entries retain their own content structure and use the catalog's localized draft, approval, and publication workflow.
- [ ] The Household privacy and platform-admin approval boundaries apply to Word Entries. A draft localization stays in review while its previously approved version remains available.
- [ ] The quiz supplies only eligible Questions. Verify that a published Word Entry appears in the appropriate catalog scope and stays outside the quiz pool.
