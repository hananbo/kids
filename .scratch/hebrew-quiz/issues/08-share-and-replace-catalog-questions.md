# 08: Share and replace catalog questions

**What to build:** Platform admins can approve Questions for the Shared Catalog. A parent can adapt a shared Question into a private replacement and choose when to restore the original for their Household.

**Blocked by:** 04 (Resume runs and withdraw published versions)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A parent-created Question stays private unless a platform admin approves it for the Shared Catalog. A parent can review and publish private content within their own Household.
- [ ] Editing a shared Question creates a private copy. Publishing that copy replaces the original in the Household's quiz pool while the Shared Catalog version remains available to other authorized Households.
- [ ] Withdrawing the private replacement keeps the original out of the Household's pool until the parent explicitly restores it. An eligible restored original is available for new runs.
- [ ] Coverage uses separate Household fixtures to verify private access, platform approval, replacement scope, withdrawal, and explicit restoration through Filament and the quiz pool boundary.
