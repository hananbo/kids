# Kids Learning Platform

The platform curates learning content for children to use across games.

## Language

**Household**  
The group a parent manages. It contains the household’s child profiles and private learning content.

**Parent**  
An adult who signs in to manage a household and review its learning content.

**Child Profile**  
A child’s identity within a household, with progress tracked separately for each game. A child profile has no email requirement.

**Shared Catalog**  
The collection of curated questions and word entries available across households.

**Question**  
A prompt with an expected answer. The prompt and its answers can use text, numbers, photos, video or audio.

**Distractor**  
An incorrect choice offered alongside the correct answer to a multiple-choice question.

**Word Entry**  
A word with its meaning and any hints used in word games.

**Subject**  
A topic used to categorize questions. A question can belong to multiple subjects.

**Target Age**  
The age from 1 to 18 for which a question is intended.

**Learning Age**  
The age a parent sets for choosing suitable questions for a child. Questions can target this age or a younger age.

**Difficulty**  
A rating from 1 to 10 for how challenging a question is.

**Published Version**  
The approved version of a question or word entry available for play while further edits are reviewed.

**Quiz Run**  
A single playthrough of the quiz.

**Quiz Level**  
A playable tier of the quiz. Each level has its own range of question difficulties.
